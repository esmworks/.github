# Esm Software

We build open-source software that teams can run on their own terms: on their own server, with
their data in their own database, and with AI assistants working inside the tools rather than
around them.

## Mission

Make good everyday software for teams that is open, self-hostable and ready to work with AI
assistants, so that a small company gets the same tools as a large one without handing its data
to someone else.

## What we're working on

### [Leafdesk](https://github.com/esmworks/leafdesk)

An open-source workspace of pages and databases with realtime collaboration and a built-in MCP
server, so AI assistants such as Claude can search, read and edit your workspace after you
approve them.

- Pages and databases shown as table, board, calendar, gallery, timeline, map, chart, dashboard or form
- Realtime editing with live cursors, and offline work
- AI chat, AI properties and agents that work on database rows, with the model of your choice
- Teamspaces, permissions, SSO, SCIM, two-step verification and an audit log
- One `docker compose up` on your own server, or the desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux
- AGPL-3.0, in 24 languages
